Calendar Blockify

Share your availability without revealing event details. All busy blocks simply show as Unavailable.

How to Get Your Calendar ICS URL
1

Open Google Calendar Settings

Go to Google Calendar and click the gear icon, then "Settings".

2

Select Your Calendar

In the left sidebar under "Settings for my calendars", click on the calendar you want to share.

3

Find "Secret address in iCal format"

Scroll down to "Integrate calendar" section. Look for "Secret address in iCal format" and copy the URL.

Note: Keep this URL private - anyone with it can see your calendar events.

4

Add to Calendar Blockify

Paste the ICS URL below, give it a name, and click "Add". You can add multiple calendars.
